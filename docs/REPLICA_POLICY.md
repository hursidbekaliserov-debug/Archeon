# NaqshLab Replica & Heritage Integrity Policy

## 1. Statement of Principle
NaqshLab produces high-fidelity architectural replicas of historic Uzbek glazed tiles (*kashi*) and carved plaster (*ganch*). Under no circumstances does NaqshLab produce, certify, or facilitate the creation of counterfeits, forged antiquities, or deceptive decorative pieces.

## 2. Mandatory Replica Marking (Rule R1)
Every physical object manufactured via NaqshLab workflows and every associated customer-facing document must visibly, indelibly, and prominently bear the official replica statement:
- **Uzbek**: `NUSXA — ASL EMAS`
- **English**: `REPLICA — NOT AN ORIGINAL`

### Physical Application:
1. **Kashi (Ceramic)**: Before the bisque firing stage, the piece serial number and the statement `NUSXA — ASL EMAS` must be debossed or inscribed into the unglazed ceramic reverse. Workshop operators must upload a photographic proof of this inscription before the task can advance.
2. **Ganch (Carved Plaster)**: Before the plaster matrix fully sets, the serial number and `NUSXA — ASL EMAS` must be stamped or carved into the mounting backing. Photographic verification is required.

### Document Application:
Job cards, quality control inspection sheets, commercial invoice lines, contractual agreements, and public certificates must display both language forms in bold monospaced text.

## 3. Strict Ban on Artificial Ageing (Rule R2)
The NaqshLab platform, data model, APIs, and workshop guidelines strictly prohibit:
- Artificial distressing, chipping, or tumbling to simulate centuries of wear.
- Patina washes, simulated soil stains, smoke deposits, or faux degradation.
- Replication, imitation, or approximation of museum accession marks, archive stamps, inventory numbers, or governmental seals.
- Any API request attempting to define an artificial ageing finish returns `422 Unprocessable Entity`.

## 4. Human Expert Authority (Rule R3)
Algorithmic symmetry extrapolation and image inpainting proposals are advisory only. No reconstructed model can enter the production pipeline without explicit verification, review, and cryptographic signature by an accredited `EXPERT`.

## 5. Seed Data & Demonstrations (Rule R4, R5, R6)
All demonstration projects, catalog entries, and seed records use procedurally generated motifs and are explicitly labeled `DEMO`. NaqshLab never fabricates client testimonials, government endorsements, or fictitious awards.
